Model, keys, and normalization

service_category (1) ──< (N) service
service  (1) ──< (N) business_service (N) >── (1) business
source   (1) ──< (N) business_service
source   (1) ──< (N) business

business_service PK = (business_id, service_id)
  • 1:N category → service (FK NOT NULL)
  • M:N business ↔ service via business_service
  • Composite PK blocks duplicate offerings (BR4)
  • Every business and offering cites a source + raw-row ref (BR3)
  • Delete business → CASCADE offerings; others RESTRICT
