Driving Directions

From the North:

  • Start by heading north on any major road, such as Interstate 90 or Interstate 94.
  • Continue north until you reach the Addison Street exit.
  • Take the exit toward Addison St.
  • Merge onto I-90 W/I-94 W.
  • Take exit 45 C for Belmont Ave toward Kedzie Ave.
  • Sharp right onto W Belmont Ave.
  • Turn left onto N Western Ave.
  • Turn right onto W Melrose St, the destination will be on the left 3235 N. Western Ave, Chicago.

From the South:

  • Begin by heading south on Interstate 90.
  • Take exit 45B for Kimball Ave toward 3400 W.
  • Use any lane to turn right onto N Kimball Ave.
  • Turn left onto W Belmont Ave.
  • Turn left onto N Western Ave.
  • Turn right onto W Melrose St, the destination will be on the left 3235 N. Western Ave, Chicago.

From the East:

  • Start by heading east on any major road, such as Belmont Ave.
  • Continue east until you reach N Western Ave, turn left.
  • Turn right onto W Melrose St.
  • On the left, you reach your destination at 3235 N. Western Ave. in Chicago.

From the West:

  • Begin by heading west on any major road, such as Addison Street or Irving Park Road.
  • Continue west until you reach Western Ave.
  • Turn left onto Western Ave.
  • Continue south on Western Ave until you reach your destination at 3235 N. Western Ave. in Chicago.

Major Highways and Points of Reference:

Interstate 90 (Kennedy Expressway) - runs east-west through Chicago.

Interstate 94 (Dan Ryan Expressway) - runs north-south through Chicago.

Addison Street - major east-west road that passes through the north side of Chicago.

Western Ave - major north-south road that passes through the north side of Chicago.

Wrigley Field - the famous baseball stadium located near the intersection of Addison St and Clark St.

Roscoe Village - a trendy neighborhood located near the intersection of Roscoe St and Western Ave.

Belmont Ave - major east-west road that intersects with Western Ave.

Lincoln Square - a vibrant neighborhood located near the intersection of Lincoln Ave and Lawrence Ave.

Directions provided for reference. Ensure you consult with a trusted navigation system and obey all traffic laws while driving.
