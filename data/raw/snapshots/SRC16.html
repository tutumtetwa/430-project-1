Frequently Asked Questions

What types of vehicles does Tuffy Chicago service?

We service all makes and models, including domestic, import, hybrid, SUV, light and medium duty trucks. 

Do you offer courtesy inspections with every service?
Yes, every customer receives a complimentary courtesy inspection covering essential components like tires, brakes, fluids, battery, belts, and exhaust. This helps identify potential issues early.
How can I schedule an appointment?
You can easily schedule an appointment using our online booking tool, by calling us at 773-900-8258, or visiting our shop during business hours. We offer flexible scheduling and extended hours for your convenience.
Are your technicians ASE-certified?

Yes! All our technicians are ASE certified and participate in continuing education courses and are skilled in handling a wide variety of vehicles with expert care.

What amenities do you offer while I wait?
Our waiting area is comfortable, featuring complimentary Wi-Fi and refreshments. We strive to make your experience pleasant and stress-free while your vehicle is being serviced.
Is transparent pricing provided for all repairs?
Yes, we believe in honest communication and transparent pricing. You will always receive a clear estimate and explanation before any work is performed on your vehicle.
