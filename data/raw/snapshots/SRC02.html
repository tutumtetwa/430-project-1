Ashland Tire & Auto: Your Vehicle's Best Friend

At Ashland Tire & Auto, we've been the trusted automotive care partner for the Lakeview and surrounding area. Founded in 1988 by the Papas family, the business is now operated by the second generation of the family. Our family-owned and operated business brings over 35 years of experience to every vehicle we service. As ASE-certified professionals, we pride ourselves on our outstanding craftsmanship and ability to work on all domestic and most foreign models.


Whether you need a routine oil change or complex mechanical repairs, our team is equipped to handle it all. We understand the importance of your vehicle in your daily life, which is why we offer comprehensive services, including tires and full mechanical diagnostics and service to keep you on the road safely and efficiently. Visit us today for your next diagnostic test!

Why Ashland Tire & Auto is Your Premier Choice

Choosing Ashland Tire & Auto means selecting a partner committed to excellence in automotive care. When you choose us, you're choosing a legacy of trust, expertise, and customer-focused service that has been the cornerstone of our business for over three decades.

Tires and Alignment Services

Your vehicle's tires are critical for safe driving. Our comprehensive tire services include sales, installation, rotation, and balancing to keep your ride smooth and secure. When it comes to alignments, our skilled technicians provide thorough inspections to ensure that your vehicle operates smoothly and efficiently, enhancing both performance and safety on the road. We use only quality parts to ensure optimal performance and safety for your vehicle.

Mechanical Services

From engine diagnostics to starter repairs, our mechanical services cover all aspects of your vehicle's performance. Our ASE-certified technicians are equipped with state-of-the-art tools and technology to accurately diagnose and efficiently repair any mechanical issue. We pride ourselves on our ability to work on all domestic and most foreign models, ensuring that no matter what you drive, you'll receive expert care.

Oil Changes

Engine oil is crucial for maintaining your vehicle's health and performance, providing essential lubrication to reduce friction between moving parts. At Ashland Tire & Auto, our expert technicians use high-quality oils and filters to ensure your engine runs smoothly and efficiently. We perform comprehensive oil change services, helping to prevent potential issues with your engine before they become costly repairs.
