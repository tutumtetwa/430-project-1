Tires & Auto Services

Your Trusted Auto Repair Shop in Chicago, IL

At Midtown Auto Repair, we provide trusted auto repair and maintenance services for cars, trucks, SUVs, and fleet vehicles in Chicago, IL. Our customers continue to trust us because we provide honest service, quality repairs, and dependable workmanship. From oil changes and brake service to engine diagnostics, wheel alignments, suspension repairs, and scheduled maintenance, our experienced technicians are here to help keep your vehicle running at its best. We take pride in offering upfront estimates, friendly customer service, and repairs done right the first time.

Need tires? We are a certified dealer of Goodyear, Dunlop, Kelly, and Cooper Tires, and we stock quality tires for many vehicle types and budgets. Whether you need new tires, diagnostics, battery replacement, or full-service auto repair, Midtown Auto Repair is ready to help. Browse our latest specials, read reviews from our satisfied customers, or stop by today and see why so many Chicago drivers choose Midtown Auto Repair for reliable automotive service.
