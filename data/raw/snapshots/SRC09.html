Only 2% of all automotive technicians in the United States are female. It’s Mechanista’s mission to solve the 2% problem by being the country’s only nonprofit automotive garage that offers training and real-world experience to the next generation of female and nonbinary automotive technicians.

mission

Mechanista is a nonprofit automotive garage that provides professional automotive training to 16-24 year-old female and nonbinary students through a paid, supportive apprenticeship program.

SERVICES

Mechanista will offer transparency and open communication in every service.

Our standard oil change and inspection includes:

  • Engine Oil Drain and Replace 

  • Engine Oil Filter Change

  • Tire Pressure Adjustment

  • Windshield Washer Refill

  • Battery Health Analysis Test

  • Engine Air Filter Check

  • Cabin Air Filter Check

  • Antifreeze Reservoir Level Check

  • Brake Fluid Reservoir Level Check

  • Power Steering Fluid Level Check

  • Wiper Blades Check

  • Exterior Lights Check

  • Transmission Fluid Level Check

  • Tire Tread Depth Check & Wear Inspection

We also offer:

  • Engine Air Filter Replacement

  • Cabin Air Filter Replacement

  • Battery Service and Replacement

  • Light Bulb Replacement

  • Wiper Blade Replacement 

  • Tire Rotation

  • Brake Pad & Rotor Replacement

  • Diagnostic Services

GET TO KNOW US.

  • Mechanista is a nonprofit automotive garage and training program for female and nonbinary 16-24 year-olds. Students receive hands-on and virtual reality training through a paid, supportive apprenticeship, registered with the U.S. Department of Labor.

  • Yes! Right this way, please

  • Our professionally trained automotive technician will service your car with the assistance of our apprentices. Apprentices will not be working alone, ever.

  • Mechanista is nestled in the heart of Ravenswood at 4526 N. Ravenswood Ave., between Sunnyside and Wilson. The entrance is connected to the alley directly behind (west of) the shop.

  • Yes please! As a non-profit, tax-exempt organization, donations are 100% tax deductible. (Any fees paid to service your car, though, are not tax-deductible since you are receiving something in exchange for what you pay.)

    Donations are used to pay our Program Manager, who recruits youth, nurtures our partnerships with schools and community centers, and provides individual case management to our youth.

  • We don’t currently have any volunteer opportunities, but please join our mailing list to be notified of any changes.
